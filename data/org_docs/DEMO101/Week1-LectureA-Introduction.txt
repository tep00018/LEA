# Week 1: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

## What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to computer systems that can perform tasks typically requiring human intelligence. These tasks include:
- Visual perception
- Speech recognition
- Decision-making
- Language translation

## Types of AI

### 1. Narrow AI (Weak AI)
Designed to perform specific tasks. Examples:
- Voice assistants (Siri, Alexa)
- Recommendation systems (Netflix, Amazon)
- Facial recognition

### 2. General AI (Strong AI)
Hypothetical AI with human-level intelligence across all domains. This does not yet exist.

### 3. Superintelligent AI
Theoretical AI that surpasses human intelligence. This is a topic of ongoing debate and research.

## AI vs Machine Learning

**Artificial Intelligence (AI)**: The broader concept of machines being able to carry out tasks in a "smart" way.

**Machine Learning (ML)**: A subset of AI that enables machines to learn from data without being explicitly programmed.

Think of it this way:
- AI is the goal (intelligent machines)
- ML is the method (learning from data)

## Key Concepts
- **Algorithm**: A set of rules or instructions for solving a problem
- **Training Data**: Data used to teach the machine learning model
- **Model**: The output of the machine learning algorithm after training
